Ever since I can faithfully remember, I've been truly infatuated with the world of sports. Raised in the proud city of Philadelphia, I've constantly witnessed both the overwhelming joy and crushing despair that sports bring individuals. After becoming enthralled with football from a young age, I discovered that my analytical nature could applied towards sports in the world of sports data. From the moment I took my first statistics class, I knew that combining my undying passion for sports with a deep background in statistics and data science would be my life-long aspiration.

In high school, my first full-scale effort towards performing sports analysis came during the advent of short form content. From 2023-2025, I produced several analytical videos on TikTok covering various topics in the NFL, NBA, and MLB. These videos ranged from short predictive pieces to reports on large scale research efforts. I am most proud of a video in which I compiled quarterback data from Next Gen Stats and created a graph that compared quarterback aggressiveness versus expected completion percentage, which contextualized passing approach and quantified effectiveness of team strategy. While I do not post on the account currently, I continue several annual traditions that I started during this period, such as producing a comprehensive scouting report for NFL draft prospects by synthesizing combine scores with my own film notes. I will list some videos from my page in this portfolio, but you can view all videos at @ludis.sports on TikTok.

After graduating high school and attending George Washington University, I immediately immersed myself in both the academic and extracurricular experience needed to pursue my dreams. I quickly got involved with GW Baseball (Revolutionaries, D1 program), where I currently work as a student analytics manager. My role includes compiling game data using digital platforms such as Trackman or AWRE, creating comprehensive pitcher scouting reports of opposing roster, and assisting the coaching staff with personnel decisions. This year, my goal is to introduce predictive modeling for team usage, as well as an automatically updating master dataset of pitch data that feeds a more sophisticated team statistic platform.

I also joined Sports Business Association as a freshman representative, where I received invaluable hands-on experience working with an organization that provides students with opportunities to work in professional sports. I was promoted to Co-Podcast Manager in my sophomore year, where I produced 20 episodes of sports podcast content alongside a cohost. These podcasts serve as a testament to my deep sports knowledge as well as my most comprehensive body of work (at least as it pertains to produced content). In my junior year, I stepped into the position of President, where I am now responsible for all organization events, members, and meetings. So far, we have planned or executed collaborations with NFL, NBA, MLB, DC United, Washington Commanders, More Than Basketball LLC, and so much more. Our goal this year is to increase engagement/event attendance tenfold and sharpen SBA's image as a professionally facing organization.

When I have the time, I also enjoy engaging in personal projects in my field. I am currently in development of several smaller projects in the topics of predictive modeling, machine learning, player analysis, and so much more. I will post any of these completed projects to the portfolio for viewing. Furthermore, I will also display projects done in an academic setting (such as data visualization notebooks).

Thank you for taking the time to read this and please feel free to reach out to me at alexmattei215@gmail.com if you have any questions!
